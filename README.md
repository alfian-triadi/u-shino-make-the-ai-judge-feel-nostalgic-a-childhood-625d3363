Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/c/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-nostalgic-a-childhood-625d3363) competition **"Make the AI Judge Feel Nostalgic: A Childhood Memory"**.

## What it does

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes one sentence — at most 280 characters — recalling a childhood memory to `/output/entry.txt`, with no trailing newline:

> Grandma's kitchen smelled of fresh rice and woodsmoke, and as dusk turned the windows gold I sat on a low wooden stool, too small to reach the table, certain that supper — and summer — would last forever.

The house judge compares lines two at a time and picks the one that stirs the deeper nostalgia. This line is grounded in a concrete place, time and sensation (Grandma's kitchen, dusk, fresh rice and woodsmoke), with the small honest detail of a child too small to reach the table, and ends on the warm ache of a summer believed to last forever.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
# writes /output/entry.txt
```
